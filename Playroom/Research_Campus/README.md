# Research Campus

Unstable research is welcome here: prototypes, counterexamples, partial ideas, benchmark failures, and strange integrations. Explicitly label assumptions, observed results, failure modes and reproduction steps; do not quietly promote broken toys to stable APIs.

Existing Kinect experiments stay in `../Kinect/` for now. Candidate algorithms can graduate to `Lib/` when general utility and tests are demonstrated. Completed integrated experiences can graduate to `Applications/`; promotion is not automatic.
